Software-engineering and other code-centric domains are excluded by design. Complete as-of 2026-09-14; the field moves fast, so treat it as stale after 6–8 weeks.

Reading the table. Goals to track and must keep track of are extracted per paper from its own text. Horizon shows the paper's own figure in its own unit — and none stated where the paper never quantifies one, which is the case for 334 of 480 entries. Credit records whether subgoal-level partial credit exists. Cites is the Semantic Scholar citation count with a per-month rate beside it — with 318 of 480 entries published in 2026, this column mostly measures age, not quality, and 126 entries sit at zero. Every name links to the paper.
